## 💡 Design Highlights

### Color Psychology
- **Obsidian**: Authority, sophistication, power
- **Gold**: Premium, luxury, success
- **Ivory**: Warmth, elegance, approachability
- **Emerald**: Trust, verification, success
- **Platinum**: Modern, technical, precision

### Typography Hierarchy
- **Display (Playfair Display)**: Headings, hero text
- **Body (Inter)**: Content, descriptions
- **Mono (JetBrains Mono)**: Technical data, codes

### Animation Principles
- **Purposeful**: Every animation serves a function
- **Smooth**: Cubic-bezier easing for natural motion
- **Subtle**: Not distracting, enhances experience
- **Consistent**: Same timing across components
