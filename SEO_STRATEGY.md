# SEO Strategy - GadiBazar Nepal

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the comprehensive SEO strategy for GadiBazar, Nepal's trusted vehicle marketplace. The strategy focuses on technical SEO foundation, content optimization, and sustainable organic growth.

---

## 1. Core SEO Objectives

### Primary Goals
1. **Indexability**: Ensure all public pages are properly indexed by search engines
2. **Visibility**: Rank for high-intent vehicle-related keywords in Nepal
3. **Traffic**: Drive qualified organic traffic from buyers and sellers
4. **Authority**: Build domain authority through quality content and technical excellence
5. **Conversion**: Convert organic traffic into leads, listings, and transactions

### Target Metrics (12 Months)
- Organic traffic: 50,000+ monthly visitors
- Indexed pages: 500+ quality pages
- Top 10 rankings: 100+ keywords
- Domain authority: 30+
- Organic conversions: 5% of traffic

---

## 2. Target Audience & Search Intent

### Primary Audiences

#### 1. Vehicle Buyers
- **Intent**: Find and purchase vehicles
- **Keywords**: "used cars Nepal", "buy car Kathmandu", "second hand bike Nepal"
- **Journey**: Search → Browse → Compare → Inspect → Purchase

#### 2. Vehicle Sellers
- **Intent**: List and sell vehicles
- **Keywords**: "sell my car Nepal", "list vehicle for sale", "car selling service"
- **Journey**: Search → List → Inspect → Sell

#### 3. Vehicle Researchers
- **Intent**: Research vehicles before purchase
- **Keywords**: "car inspection Nepal", "vehicle passport", "EV battery check"
- **Journey**: Search → Learn → Verify → Decide

#### 4. Service Seekers
- **Intent**: Find inspection, finance, insurance services
- **Keywords**: "vehicle inspection service", "car finance Nepal", "vehicle insurance"
- **Journey**: Search → Compare → Book → Use

## 4. Site Architecture

### URL Structure
```
/                                    Homepage
/cars                                All cars category
/motorcycles                         All motorcycles category
/electric-vehicles                   All EVs category
/cars/toyota                         Make page
/cars/toyota/fortuner                Model page
/cars/kathmandu                      Location page
/vehicle/[slug]                      Individual vehicle
/vehicle-passport/[id]               Passport page
/vehicle-inspection                  Inspection service
/ev-battery-check                    EV battery service
/sell-my-car                         Sell service
/valuation                           Valuation tool
/blog                                Blog hub
/blog/[slug]                         Blog post
/about                               About page
/contact                             Contact page
```

### Hierarchy
```
Level 1: Homepage
Level 2: Categories (Cars, Motorcycles, EVs)
Level 3: Makes (Toyota, Hyundai, etc.)
Level 4: Models (Fortuner, Creta, etc.)
Level 5: Individual Vehicles
```

### Internal Linking Strategy
- Every vehicle page links to make/model pages
- Every make/model page links to related models
- Location pages link to vehicles in that location
- Service pages link to relevant vehicle pages
- Blog posts link to relevant service and vehicle pages

## 9. Technical Performance

- Minification of CSS/JS
